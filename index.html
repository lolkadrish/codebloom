BEFORE YOUR FIRST LINE07 / 08

A few good questions.

Do I need any experience?+

No. Start with the first stage: input, output, and simple variables. Each new tool is introduced before you use it.

Do I need to install anything?+

The course editor and exercise checks run in your browser. Later topics also explain working with your own project files.

Can I learn without an account?+

Yes. Guest progress stays in this browser. Sign in when you want to sync your progress across devices.

Can I change the explanation language?+

Use the EN / RU / UK selector at the top. Your programming language and your explanation language are separate choices.
